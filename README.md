# Nexus Rush

Nexus Rush is a turn-based tactical card game played on a terrain grid.

The project is being developed as a separate game from Dominion Rush. It keeps the fast, readable mobile presentation, but gameplay is based on deliberate unit movement, attack range, terrain, structures, spells and destructible Nexus objectives.

## Current prototype goals

- 20-card decks, maximum 2 copies of the same card.
- Card types: Units, Structures and one-shot Spells.
- Each card has its own mana cost.
- Mana is banked between turns.
- Mana income starts at +2 per turn and increases by +1 every 5 turns.
- Units use Life, Movement, Range, Attack and optional abilities.
- Movement is orthogonal only.
- Terrain is hand-designed and mirrored for competitive fairness.
- Horizontal mode: 2 Nexus per player; destroying either wins.
- Vertical mode: 1 Nexus per player.
- Nexus base Life: 10.

The first playable prototype will use two starter archetypes:
- Arcane: spells, ranged pressure and control.
- Bastion: paladins, melee pressure and limited ranged support.
